# AI Product Ops That Ship

Teams do not struggle because they lack ideas. They struggle because they lack an operating system.

## Start With A Weekly Value Loop

- Define one user-visible outcome each week.
- Reduce scope until it can ship in five days.
- Review with real usage, not internal opinions.

## Keep AI Work Boring In Production

The best AI systems are intentionally boring where it counts: observability, fallback behavior, and audit trails.

## What To Standardize Early

1. Prompt review checklist
2. Evaluation dataset by feature
3. Rollback runbook

Shipping AI safely is less about model novelty and more about operational discipline.
