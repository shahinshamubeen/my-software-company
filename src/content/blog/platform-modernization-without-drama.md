# Platform Modernization Without Drama

Big rewrites fail because they ask the business to wait too long for value.

## Prefer Thin Vertical Slices

Choose one customer journey, rebuild it end to end, and release behind a flag.

## Keep Legacy And New Running Side By Side

- Route a small percentage of traffic first.
- Compare error rate and latency in real time.
- Expand only when metrics are consistently better.

## Measure The Right Outcomes

Track lead time, change failure rate, and customer-visible latency. Those three reveal whether modernization is actually working.
